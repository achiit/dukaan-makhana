From my grandfather's pond to your kitchen shelf

My grandfather grew makhana in the ponds around Sitamarhi long before it became a "superfood" anywhere else. I grew up watching the seeds being harvested by hand in the monsoon and roasted in small batches through winter — that's the only way I know how to do it.

We started Vrat Bhog Makhana a little over two years ago because too much of what's sold as "premium makhana" in shops is old stock, over-roasted, or bulked up with fillers. We roast in small batches, pack quickly, and ship the same week — nothing sits in a warehouse.

If you're buying for a vrat, a festival gift, or just your evening chai, I'd rather you message me directly on WhatsApp than order blind — I'll tell you honestly which pack suits your need.

  • Sourced directly from Mithila's ponds
  • Roasted fresh in small weekly batches
  • Zero preservatives, zero artificial colour
  • Sattvic vrat recipes tested by the family
  • Packed and dispatched within 48 hours
